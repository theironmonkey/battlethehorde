    Battle log

      Challenge quick reference
      • Bring up to three different Heroes. The player can attack or target the Horde from the first turn.
      • For each life the Horde would lose, mill one card. Artifact rewards trigger when those cards reach the graveyard from any zone.
      • At the Horde’s precombat main phase, reveal two cards plus one for each artifact in play when that phase starts. Cast the revealed cards.
      • Make choices for the Horde. Ignore its draws, discards, and impossible actions. Send Horde permanents to its graveyard if they would move to another zone besides its library.
      • Win when the Horde has an empty library and zero creatures. Lose when your life reaches zero or you fail a required draw.
